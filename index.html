What is a side hustle?

A side hustle is any work you do on the side, outside your main job or studies, to earn extra income. It can be small, like selling things online, or it can grow into a full business over time.

Most good side hustles share three things: a low startup cost, flexible hours and a skill or service people already pay for.

Earnings vary a lot from person to person. Nobody can promise you a fixed income, so be careful of anyone who does.

How to pick the right side hustle

  1. List your skills. Think about what you are good at, even simple things like cooking, design or writing.
  2. Check your time. Be honest about how many hours a week you can give.
  3. Keep startup costs low. Start with what you already have before you buy anything.
  4. Test before you commit. Try one idea for a few weeks, then decide whether to continue.

Scams to avoid

Be careful of any offer that asks you to pay first and promises big returns with no work. Common warning signs:

  • "Guaranteed" income or "get rich fast" claims
  • Jobs that ask you to pay a fee to start
  • Requests to send money, or to receive and forward payments for strangers
  • Pressure to decide immediately

Your 7-day launch plan

  1. Day 1: Choose one idea. Pick the one that fits your skills and time.
  2. Day 2: Define your offer. Write one sentence: who you help and how.
  3. Day 3: Set a price. Check what others charge and start fairly.
  4. Day 4: Set up your profile. Create an account on a platform or social media page.
  5. Day 5: Make a sample. Prepare one example of your work to show.
  6. Day 6: Tell people. Message friends, family and groups you are part of.
  7. Day 7: Get your first customer. Offer a small discount for your first order and ask for feedback.

Finding your first customers

  • Start with people you know, since they trust you most.
  • Post examples of your work regularly on social media.
  • Join online groups where your customers spend time, and help before you promote.
  • Ask every happy customer for a review or a referral.
